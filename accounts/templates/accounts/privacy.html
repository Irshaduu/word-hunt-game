Privacy

Word Hunt is a free game made by one person. It keeps as little about you as it can.

What it keeps

  • Your game name, and when you joined.
  • Your PIN, if you use one, stored only as a one-way hash.
  • Your Google account ID and the link to your Google profile picture, if you sign in with Google. Not your email address: the game never stores it.
  • Your games: places, words found, seconds left, and who you played with.
  • Your friends, friend requests and the people you block.
  • A sign-in cookie, so you stay signed in. There are no ad or tracking cookies.

Voice

Voice chat goes straight between players' devices, or through Cloudflare's relay when a direct connection isn't possible. It is never recorded or stored. Room text chat isn't stored either.

Who sees it

Signed-in players can see your name, picture, stats and recent games. Nothing is sold or shared with advertisers. The game runs on Render, with its database on Neon.

Deleting it

Delete your account from your profile page, and everything above goes with it at once. Demo guest accounts delete themselves after a day.

Questions

Open an issue on the game's GitHub page.
